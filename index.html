Quality ribbon + Q alarm
The upper ribbon is mean Phred quality at each read position. The dashed Q-alarm line is a chosen warning threshold, not a pass/fail verdict.
Active readout
Shows the playing codon, its mean Q, highest-priority event, sampled read/base position, nine-base motif ID and occurrence count.

CleanNo local rule fired; it does not certify the read or sample.

WarningOne or more codon Q values fall enough below the Q alarm to become audible.

FractureA strong low-Q warning (mean below Q20 or high severity), not a broken or mutated biological codon.

N callThe codon contains N or an unsupported sequence symbol normalised to N.

AdapterA recognised adapter-like signature occurs in the local context; this is not a full contamination test.

HomopolymerThe local nine-base motif contains at least five identical bases in a row.

Low complexityThe local nine-base motif has unusually low sequence diversity.

Motif echoes
The count is how often that motif occurs in sampled windows; three or more occurrences may add an octave echo. It does not explain the cause.
MAP fingerprint
The eight-character label identifies the sampled sequence view for comparison. It is deterministic, but not a cryptographic file checksum.
